## Key Observations

1. **Systematic over-counting**: The model consistently over-estimates visit counts, especially at longer time windows
2. **Error scales with time**: MAE increases from 1.6 days (screening) to 16.3 days (12 months)
3. **No complexity benefit**: Simple schedules don't perform better than complex ones
4. **Near-term is manageable**: Screening and 1-month windows have ~90% clinical accuracy
5. **Long-term fails**: 6+ month windows have only 12-17% clinical accuracy
