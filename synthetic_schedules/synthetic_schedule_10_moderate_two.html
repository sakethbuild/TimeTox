Arm A (Durvalumab + Chemoradiation): Visits on Days 1 of each 7-day cycle for 2 months
Arm B (Chemoradiation Alone): Visits on Days 1 of each 7-day cycle for 2 months

a Screening: All assessments must be completed within 7 days prior to C1D1. Screening may require 1-2 visits.

b Treatment Phase: 7-day cycles. Arm A: Days 1; Arm B: Days 1.

c Labs: CBC includes WBC with differential, hemoglobin, hematocrit, platelets, ANC.

d CMP: Includes sodium, potassium, chloride, bicarbonate, BUN, creatinine, glucose, calcium.

e EOT Visit: End of treatment visit 30-42 days after last dose or at early discontinuation.

f Follow-Up: Month 9 and 12 visits for disease recurrence and survival monitoring.

g Adverse Events: Assessed continuously from first dose through 30 days after last dose.

h Imaging: CT scans at screening, EOT, and Month 12 follow-up per RECIST 1.1.
